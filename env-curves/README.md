# Reference Screenshots: env-curves

Reference visual verification screenshots for the frozen curve road world (`CurveRoadWorld.ts`):

1. **01_spawn_view.png**: Spawn view behind car ($s = 20\text{ m}$). Shows the 8m road ribbon, darker edge strips, continuous double-rail wooden fence line at 6.0m on both sides, flat ground plane, and horizon sky.
2. **02_fence_road_straight.png**: Low-angle close-up of the fence and road ribbon along the Section 1 straight, displaying fence post spacing (every 4m), rail height, and road surface boundaries.
3. **03_hairpin_chase_camera.png**: Hairpin turn (Section 6, $R = 25\text{ m}$, $120^\circ$ right) from chase camera mid-corner, illustrating vehicle body roll and fence tracking around the constant-radius arc.
4. **04_chicane.png**: Chicane sequence (Section 8 left $R=80\text{ m}$, $45^\circ$ into Section 9 right $R=80\text{ m}$, $45^\circ$) showing smooth transition between opposing constant-radius arcs.
5. **05_far_end_barrier.png**: Solid hazard red/white striped end barrier at $s = 1470\text{ m}$ closing off the track and preventing out-of-bounds traversal.
6. **06_straight_high_speed.png**: Vehicle traveling at ~120 mph on the first straight with chase camera view.
